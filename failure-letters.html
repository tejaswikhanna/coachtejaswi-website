Why Failure Letters Exists

Modern life teaches us to hide our cracks — to move quickly past uncertainty, grief, exhaustion, and failure. Failure Letters is an attempt to slow down and sit honestly with those moments instead. Through stories, reflections, and emotional essays, this space explores what happens when we stop treating failure as proof of inadequacy and begin seeing it as transformation, redirection, and return.
