You are not failing. You are being redirected.

I still remember the day I got the rejection email. It was a project I'd poured my heart into for months — sleepless nights, complete dedication, and all my hopes compressed into one proposal. Reading those words felt like being told I wasn't good enough.

Why this exists

Failure Letters was born from the realization that most people carry invisible collapses silently. Rejections, emotional exhaustion, uncertainty, ambition, loneliness, overthinking — modern life teaches us to move quickly past them. This space exists to slow down instead. Not to escape pain, but to understand it more honestly.
