About Tejaswi

Welcome. I'm Tejaswi Khanna — a writer exploring the quiet spaces between failure and growth, collapse and rebuilding, silence and transformation.

Through books, reflections, and emotional storytelling, my work invites readers to slow down, look inward, and rediscover meaning in moments that often feel broken.

I believe that our deepest struggles are not interruptions to life — they are invitations into a more honest version of ourselves.

Philosophy

Growth Through Failure

Failure is not the opposite of success. It is often the beginning of self-understanding.

Presence Over Perfection

Transformation happens quietly through awareness, compassion, and honest reflection.

Kintsugi Spirit

Our cracks and scars can become the very places where meaning and beauty emerge.
